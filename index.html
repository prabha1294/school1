About us

History

Started in 1998 with 120 students in 6 classrooms, Greenfield now runs a 5-acre campus with 1,250 students.

Mission

To give every child a strong education and the character to use it well.

Vision

Confident, curious and compassionate citizens of the world.

Chairman's message

Schools are built on trust. We keep earning yours every day.

Management team

Chairman, Principal, two Vice Principals and four Section Heads.

Accreditations

CBSE affiliated. ISO 9001:2015 certified. Member of the National Green School Network.

Academics


Curriculum

CBSE with project-based learning and a second language from Grade 1.

Teaching method

Concept first, then practice, then real-life application.

Examinations

Two terms, regular unit tests and continuous assessment.
